About the project

A label made
for everyday choice.

Iz Korzinki is the private-label trademark of the Korzinka supermarket chain, created for customers who value high quality, reliability and confidence in their choice. The brand brings food and non-food products together as an accessible alternative to A-brands.

From eggs to everyday essentials

A system that
works across the shelf.

Starting with the egg carton, the collection shows how Iz Korzinki’s identity adapts across fresh produce, pantry staples and everyday essentials. A consistent signature, colour and product imagery keep every format instantly recognisable.
