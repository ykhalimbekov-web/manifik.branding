The brief and the idea

A standout taste.
A character of its own.

For this concept, we imagined a new sparkling-drink brand. The task was to create a recognisable identity that feels fresh and stands apart from familiar packaging.

Less rules.
More of your own wave.

At the centre of Onda is the joy of being yourself. The wave became the key device, connecting the name, graphics and rhythm of the pack. Colour separates the flavours and brings the range together.

Brand idea

Catch the taste.
Keep your own wave.

Light, lively, immediate.
Like the first sip.

Freshness in
every detail.

Packaging

Two flavours.
One wave.

Lime & mint / Yuzu & basil

Wave graphics unite the range. Shifting colour accents distinguish the flavours while keeping the brand recognisable.

Outcome

A character that
holds together.

The name, logo, colour system and packaging concept come together as one clear Onda identity. This project shows Manifik’s approach to a brand, from its idea to its visual expression.

An original practice concept. This is not client work and does not claim commercial results.
